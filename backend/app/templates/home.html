What you get

On every free page

  • Your public page

    Your story, services, licenses and insurance, a photo and video portfolio, and customer reviews — with a quote form that turns visitors into leads.

  • Handoffs & vacation coverage

    Booked solid, or finally taking a week off? Hand jobs to members you trust and pick up work when you're slow. Vacations lift the handoff caps.

  • Lead marketplace

    Homeowners post jobs and local contractors bid. Free plans get 3 bids a month; Full bids without a cap.

  • Community & Break Room

    Posts, supplies, hiring, mentoring and meetups — a community of people in the trades, not another feed of ads.

  • Pay & rates, and the cost calculator

    See what the trade charges around you, and price jobs with a calculator that keeps markup and margin straight.

Starter adds

  • The daily rundown

    Every morning, one summary of what's new — leads, messages, follow-ups due — by email, and as a text on paid plans.

  • Time tracking

    Track time on jobs in the same place you track the work.

  • YouTube auto-posting

    Connect your channel once, and the project videos you pick post to YouTube after a quick confirm.

Full adds

  • Customer payments

    Take card and bank payments from customers through Stripe, on a pay page made for the job.

Just starting out?

Brand new, or thinking about it? This is a good first step:

  • Your free page is your first storefront — no website needed to look established from day one.
  • Bid on marketplace jobs to land your first customers — the free plan includes 3 bids a month.
  • The community has a lane just for you — Starting Out: starter tips from people who've done it, meetups with other new owners, and experienced members who want to mentor you.
  • Pay & rates and the cost calculator help you price your first jobs right.
  • Established shops hand off overflow work here — that's how new shops get fed.
