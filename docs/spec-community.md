# Trade Circle community expansion spec
Recorded October 23, 2026. Consolidates and replaces the earlier feature-addition prompts. The base product spec is [`spec.md`](spec.md); build status is in [`roadmap.md`](roadmap.md).

Trade Circle is mainly a contractor social network for community, reputation, learning and brand building. Leads and the lead marketplace are supporting features that may grow. Extend existing components rather than duplicating them, and keep the phone experience quick.

Priority: 1. Supplies & Vendors. 2. Guided project walkthroughs. 3. The rest, in manageable stages.

## 1. Supplies & Vendors
- A dedicated page: Member Recommendations on top, Supplier Directory below.
- Members ask where to find supplies, better prices, local stock or reliable suppliers; recommend suppliers from firsthand experience; discuss price, quality, availability, delivery, service and returns; suggest suppliers to add; connect posts to listings.
- Filters: trade or supply category, city or ZIP, distance, and suppliers that ship to the area. Questions carry a location.
- Seed a few established suppliers with confirmed facts only (name, official website, locations, supplies, delivery, business-account requirements where known). Never invent details.
- Starter listings are directory entries, not endorsements, with no preferred placement. Keep platform facts apart from member opinions. Any verification label states exactly what was checked; being well known is not "trusted" or "verified".
- Recommendations stay reachable from listings. Positive and negative experiences are allowed. No receipts or proof required. Member reviews are never labeled as verified facts.
- Reporting and moderation for spam, harassment, threats and suspected fabricated content.
- **Removal requests:** confirm the request comes from the business or its authorized representative. Remove the listing and public identifying information (names, links, logos, photos, identifying replies) while keeping member reviews in anonymized form, with neutral text such as "Supplier details removed". Members may contact reviewers privately through normal messaging; no special vendor-reveal feature and no automatic disclosure of removed details. Flag cases that can't be meaningfully anonymized for moderator review. Promise neither anonymity nor legal immunity.

## 2. Guided project walkthroughs with tap-to-talk
- "Share a Project": one short question at a time. What was the job? Before photos? What challenge came up? How did you handle it? Finished photos? What did you learn or would you change?
- Typing, or explicit tap-to-talk dictation with a visible recording indicator and stop control. Never listen in the background. Typing works when voice is unavailable or declined.
- Back, Skip, Save Draft and draft autosave. Most fields optional. Photos at the relevant steps.
- Assemble answers into an organized, editable post that keeps the member's meaning and invents nothing. Preview and explicit publication; never publish automatically.
- Optionally feature on the full profile as portfolio content. Comments and trade tags. Remind members not to expose customer addresses or private information in text or photos.

## 3. Private follow-up tracker
- Inquiries from Trade Circle or entered by hand from calls, texts and conversations: contact name, optional contact details, private notes, follow-up date, status (waiting, followed up, won, closed), upcoming and overdue views.
- Private to the owner, with access controls. Reuse existing inquiry features.
- Keep lead-alert rules: free contractors log in to see leads; text and email lead alerts start at the $3 tier. Don't change those rules for tracker reminders; reminder delivery and pricing is a decision to flag.

## 4. Equipment experiences
- Ask about tools and equipment; share what's used and for what, durability and problems, repairs and maintenance, and whether they'd buy it again.
- Product and model names, photos, trade tags, searchable discussions, links to supplier listings. Opinions clearly identified; seller or sponsor affiliations must be disclosed. Use the shared posting components, not a separate review system.

## 5. Lessons From the Job
- A searchable category: what happened, what worked or went wrong, how it was resolved, what would help another contractor. Short posts, photos, comments and trade tags. Reuse walkthrough components; allow a quick lesson without a full walkthrough.

## 6. Optional mentoring
- Opt in as Looking for a Mentor or Willing to Help, with topics, experience, preferred contact method and availability. Introductions through existing messaging, with recipient consent and normal blocking and reporting.
- Voluntary. Don't imply unlimited free support, guaranteed responses or platform certification of mentors. Members can pause or remove their status.

## 7. Lightweight local meetups
- Title and description; date, time and time zone; general location; host; RSVP; updates or cancellation. Location and trade filters. Reuse the feed and notification infrastructure. No large event system before there is demand.

## 8. Limited contractor handoffs
- Up to three outgoing handoffs per week in total; no more than two to the same recipient per week. Explain limits before submission, show remaining allowance and reset time, block at the limit, keep other features available.
- Vacation is the only exception: tied to the vacation setting; start date, end date and short note; up to fourteen consecutive days; normal limits lifted during it and restored automatically afterward; overlapping or back-to-back entries can't extend it past fourteen days; coverage beyond fourteen days arranged outside the platform.
- Recipients accept or decline after seeing job details. Record sender, recipient, dates, acceptance and completion. An invitation is not an acceptance.
- No permanent handoff partnerships or exclusive groups. Repeated-exchange history reviewable without automatic penalties.
- Document the weekly reset time zone and how pending, declined, cancelled and vacation-period handoffs count. Flag undefined counting rules. Enforce limits on the backend, including simultaneous requests.

## 9. Existing profile and lead features
- The landing page, Request a Quote button and full profile already cover the shareable business page; reuse them.
- Keep occasional inquiries simple. As the marketplace grows, compact previews show job type, location, requested date, photos and budget, with missing information marked. Don't assume high lead volume or duplicate dashboards.

## 10. Earlier supporting ideas (check and report gaps)
Simple onboarding checklist; available, booked and vacation statuses; why a marketplace lead matches a contractor; alert delivery history; confirmed versus self-reported profile details. No free-tier alerts or pricing changes without an explicit decision.

## Costs
Identify recurring costs for voice, storage, maps or notifications before introducing new paid services.
